Featured CAD Deep-Dive: Parametric Blender Assembly

Software: Siemens NX

Design Intent & Methodology:

This project showcases advanced Parametric Modeling within a Master Assembly context. Even without the final physical files, the model history demonstrates professional "CAD Hygiene":

  • Robust Constraints: 100% "Fully-Constrained" sketches to ensure model stability during dimension changes.
  • Complex Surfacing: Utilized Through Curves and Sweeps to develop ergonomic transitions for the pour-spout and handle.
  • DfM (Design for Manufacturing): Integrated constant-wall-thickness Shelling and draft analysis for injection molding viability.
